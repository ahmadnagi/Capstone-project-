## My positions

1. Your trainer's program probably isn't built for you. Most are one standard
   plan for everyone.
2. Being wrecked after every session isn't progress. It usually means the
   program is wrong.
3. You don't need six days a week. Three to four is enough. Rest days are when
   the muscle actually gets built.
4. The scale is a bad measure. Water, salt and a hard session move it.
   Measure by your clothes, the mirror and how you feel.
5. There's no "best six-pack exercise". Everyone has abs; the difference is
   the fat on top. Eat right, track calories, train.
